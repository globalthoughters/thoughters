About us Two friends who still like arguing about the long game — how people live, how systems work, and what the future might hold. Where the word came from Years ago, someone trying to say “thinking” came out with “thoughting” instead. It stuck. For a long time it was just an inside joke — the kind of nonsense that makes perfect sense to the people who were there. Much later, when we decided to put some of our longer thoughts into the world, the old joke felt oddly perfect. Thoughting. Thoughters. A slightly broken, slightly cheeky name for a place where two people still try to think in public without taking themselves too seriously. J Thoughter J is a strategy-minded observer whose adult life has involved moving between cities, countries and very different ways of doing things. The constant motion became a kind of education: watching how places handle growth, stress, bureaucracy and hope. He writes from the vantage point of someone who has seen familiar problems wear different clothes in different settings, and who still believes careful observation beats loud opinion. A parent, chronic notetaker, and the one who first insisted that “three centuries” was a useful unit of time to think in. R Thoughter R is a systems thinker with a sharp eye for how institutions, markets and technology behave over time. His work has taken him across strategy, technology and complex organisations, and he is drawn to the deeper patterns rather than the surface noise. Curious about how cultures, cities and institutions change, he brings a clear, rigorous perspective to Thoughters — and a healthy allergy to empty jargon. This is not a business site. It is simply two people who like thinking in public — about societies, cities and the longer arc of change — and who decided an old shared joke was good enough to name the experiment after. Thoughters
